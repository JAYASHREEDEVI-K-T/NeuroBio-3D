    Action Potential

    How electrical signals travel through a neuron — the biology of nerve impulse propagation.

    ⚡ Action Potential Stages

    Resting State (-70mV)

    Na⁺ channels closed. K⁺ slightly open. Negative charge inside the axon membrane.

    Depolarization (+40mV)

    Na⁺ channels open. Sodium rushes inside, making it positive inside.

    Repolarization

    K⁺ channels open. Potassium leaves cell. Charge returns to negative.

    Hyperpolarization

    Briefly more negative than resting. Neuron is in refractory period.

    Recovery

    Na⁺/K⁺ pump restores -70mV. Neuron ready to fire again.

    Synaptic Transmission

    How neurons communicate across the synaptic cleft using neurotransmitters.

    🔗 How Synapses Work

    1
    Action Potential Arrives
    Electrical signal reaches the axon terminal (presynaptic neuron).
    2
    Ca²⁺ Influx
    Voltage-gated calcium channels open, Ca²⁺ enters the terminal.
    3
    Vesicle Fusion
    Synaptic vesicles fuse with the membrane, releasing neurotransmitters.
    4
    Receptor Binding
    Neurotransmitters cross the cleft and bind to receptors on postsynaptic neuron.
    5
    Signal Continues or Stops
    EPSP (excitatory) or IPSP (inhibitory) generated. Neurotransmitters are reuptaken.

    Types of Neurons

    Different neurons serve distinct biological roles in the nervous system.

    Sensory Neuron

    Carries signals FROM sense organs TO the CNS (afferent). Unipolar structure.

    Afferent

    Motor Neuron

    Carries signals FROM the CNS TO muscles/glands (efferent). Multipolar structure.

    Efferent

    Interneuron

    Connects sensory & motor neurons. Found mainly in CNS. Bipolar/multipolar.

    Connector

    Purkinje Cell

    Found in cerebellum. Massive dendritic tree. Controls motor coordination.

    Cerebellum

    ⚙️ How the ML Algorithm Works
    1
    Training Data

    120 labeled electrophysiological recordings across 4 neuron classes. Each sample has 5 extracted features: amplitude, firing rate, spike width, AHP depth, and burst index.

    2
    Feature Normalization

    All features are Z-score normalized (zero mean, unit variance) so no single feature dominates the distance metric due to scale differences.

    3
    kNN Classification

    For your input vector, the algorithm computes Euclidean distance to all 120 training samples in 5D feature space, then finds the 5 nearest neighbors.

    4
    Majority Vote

    The predicted class is determined by plurality vote among the k=5 neighbors. Confidence is the fraction of neighbors voting for the winning class.
